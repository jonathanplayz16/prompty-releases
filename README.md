# Prompty by Lumly AI

**Prompty is a free Windows app that writes your AI prompts for you.** Say what you want in plain words, and Prompty writes a strong prompt, sends it to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Perplexity, and pushes back until the answer is good.

**Website and download: [lumlyai.com](https://lumlyai.com)**

## What it does
- Lives in a small bar at the top of your screen
- Asks what's missing, then writes a full prompt for the AI you use
- Reads the answer and asks again when it misses something
- **Fix this:** select any text and press Ctrl+Alt+F to rewrite it in place
- **Roast my screen:** your agent teases you about what's on your screen
- A cute agent for every AI, with hats to collect (1 in 100 is born shiny)
